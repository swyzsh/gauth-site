Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-11.

The apps on gauth.swyzsh.com are for personal use. They run on my own computers and sign in to my own Google account.

What they access

rclone reads and writes files in my Google Drive so I can upload and download them. Files go between my computer and Google, nowhere else.

What they collect

Nothing. There's no server, no analytics and no tracking, and no data is shared or sold. The Google sign-in token stays in a config file on my computer. This site sets no cookies.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
